# Nimbus Labs Employee Handbook

## Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Unused vacation days can be carried over,
up to a maximum of 8 days, into the next year. Sick leave is separate: employees get 12 days of paid sick leave annually,
and a medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## Remote Work
Nimbus Labs follows a hybrid model. Employees must be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. All other days may be
worked remotely. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a department head and are reviewed every six months.

## Expense Reimbursement
Business travel expenses are reimbursed within 15 working days of submitting receipts through the Expensify portal.
Meals during travel are capped at 40 USD per day. Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy.
The annual learning budget is 1,200 USD per employee and can be used for courses, books and conferences.
