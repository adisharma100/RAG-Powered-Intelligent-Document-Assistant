# Nimbus Cloud Storage - Product FAQ

## Plans and Pricing
Nimbus offers three plans. The Free plan includes 5 GB of storage. The Pro plan costs 9 USD per month and includes
2 TB of storage. The Business plan costs 18 USD per user per month and includes unlimited storage, audit logs and
priority support.

## Refund Policy
Monthly subscriptions can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Annual subscriptions can be refunded within 30 days.
Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## File Limits
The maximum size of a single uploaded file is 50 GB on the Pro and Business plans and 2 GB on the Free plan.
Deleted files stay in the trash for 30 days before they are permanently removed.

## Support
Free users get community support only. Pro users receive email support with a 48 hour response target. Business users
get 24/7 chat support with a 1 hour response target.
